About Vietnam Association of Fashion & Beauty

🏠 / About VAFB

"Connecting, building, and expanding Vietnam's fashion and cosmetics ecosystem together."

The fashion and cosmetics sectors in Vietnam target identical consumer demographics and together shape lifestyle aspirations, yet operate as separate ecosystems: fashion brands collaborate with fashion brands, while beauty companies operate within their own isolated networks.

Existing enterprise support structures — traditional trade associations and private consulting firms — serve two distinct objectives with no entity filling the void in between. VAFB is proposed as a neutral institutional platform bridging both models.

VAFB constructs shared, durable assets for the entire industry — the VAFB Accord, Live Pre-Vetted B2B Directory, Volume 0 Market Intelligence Report, and an annual Ecosystem Showcase — alongside firewalled, bespoke corporate advisory operating completely separate from research activities.

In the long term, VAFB aims to establish a permanent institutional infrastructure for Vietnam's Fashion & Beauty industry, directly supporting UN SDGs 8 and 17.

Operating Model

Building Shared Industry Assets

The VAFB Accord, Live Pre-Vetted B2B Directory, and Volume 0 Market Intelligence Report form common, accessible infrastructure that every member enterprise can leverage instead of independently building internal resources.

Firewalled Advisory Isolated from Research

Corporate strategic advisory is executed independently per client project under strict data firewalling principles, managed separately from public market research to guarantee absolute neutrality between ecosystem rivals.

Cross-Industry Activation via Annual Showcase

Inspired by international fashion weeks and lifestyle expos, the Ecosystem Showcase unites fashion brands, beauty companies, designers, manufacturers, media, and KOLs/KOCs to activate cross-sector commercial partnerships.

Establishing Sector-Wide Institutional Infrastructure

In the long run, VAFB's core functions reinforce each other within a closed-loop system — market research enhances advisory quality, while B2B matching and showcases forge new commercial partnerships — systematically erecting institutional infrastructure for the entire market.
