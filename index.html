VIETNAM ASSOCIATION OF FASHION & BEAUTY

Fashion and cosmetics in Vietnam cater to the same lifestyle consumer segment, yet operate in isolated silos. VAFB — Vietnam Association of Fashion & Beauty — serves as a neutral institutional platform combining association governance, market intelligence, strategic firewalled advisory, and business matching into a unified model, empowering local fashion brands, cosmetic enterprises, designers, and manufacturers to grow sustainably together.
